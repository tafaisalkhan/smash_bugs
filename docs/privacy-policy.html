Smash Bugs & Flies Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smash Bugs & Flies (“the Game”, “we”, “our”) handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game stores level progress, high scores, coins, stars, sound/music toggles, difficulty selection (Easy, Medium, Hard), and player mode (Child / Adult) locally on your device storage. This data is not uploaded to any private external server.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The Game integrates Google AdMob to display banner and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID), IP address, and ad performance diagnostics in compliance with Google's privacy policies. Where required, consent prompts are shown to give you control over ad personalization.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases (such as Remove Ads) are handled through Google Play Billing. We never receive or store complete payment card details.

4. Children’s Privacy

Smash Bugs & Flies is family-friendly and includes a dedicated Child Mode. We do not collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
