# Privacy Policy for Smash Bugs & Flies

**Last Updated:** November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Smash Bugs & Flies** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you play our mobile game on Android.

---

## 1. Information Collected and Stored Locally

We respect your privacy. The Game operates primarily offline with data stored locally on your device:
* **Gameplay Data:** Level progress, difficulty settings (Easy / Medium / Hard), stars earned, coins collected, win streak, and high scores.
* **User Preferences:** Sound effect volume, background music toggle, notification preferences, and selected player mode (Child / Adult).
* **Entitlements:** In-app purchase status (such as "Remove Ads").

This data is stored solely in local application storage on your device (`user://`) and is never uploaded to any proprietary personal servers.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The free version of the Game utilizes third-party advertising to support development and free gameplay.

### Google AdMob
* The Game integrates **Google AdMob** to display banner and rewarded video advertisements.
* Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:
  * Device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID).
  * IP address and approximate location data derived from network information.
  * Ad interactions, views, and performance diagnostics.
* Where legally required (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), players are presented with consent dialogs to choose between personalized and non-personalized advertising.
* For more information on how Google processes ad data, please review [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### Google Play Services
* The Game uses standard Google Play Services for distribution, automatic updates, and in-app purchase verification (e.g., Remove Ads).
* All financial transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing; we never receive or store your credit card or financial details.

---

## 3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

* **Smash Bugs & Flies** offers dedicated gameplay modes:
  * **Child Mode (Easy):** Features slower bug movements, extra drawing ink, and kid-friendly mechanics.
  * **Adult Mode (Normal / Hard):** Standard and pro challenge tiers.
* We do **not** knowingly collect, store, or solicit personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13.
* The Game does not feature chat, social messaging, camera access, microphone access, or user profile creation.
* Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise their children's mobile device usage and in-app purchases.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

The Game may request the following device permissions:
* **Internet & Network State:** Required by Google AdMob to load and display advertisements and verify Google Play purchases.
* **Post Notifications (Optional):** Used solely on Android 13+ to send optional Daily Challenge reminders if enabled in Settings. You can disable this permission at any time in the Game's settings or your device's application manager.

---

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

* Because gameplay data is saved directly on your local device, all locally saved progress is automatically removed when you clear the application's storage data or uninstall the Game from your device.
* Any data handled independently by third-party services (Google Play, AdMob) is managed according to their respective retention policies.

---

## 6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates, new features, or changing legal standards. The updated date at the top of this policy will reflect any revisions.
